## 17.4.1.15 AHB Clock Control 2 (AHBCLKCTRL2)

Offset 

<table><tr><td>Register</td><td>Offset</td></tr><tr><td>AHBCLKCTRL2</td><td>208h</td></tr></table>

## Function

The AHBCLKCTRLn registers enable the clocks of the individual modules. 

NOTE When modifying the AHBCLKCTRL registers, use the related AHBCLKCTRLSET and AHBCLKCTRLCLR registers to avoid setting or clearing bits unintentionally.
