## 62.3.1.5.2 LS or FS differential driver

The LS or FS differential drivers are essentially a pair of low-impedance pullup and pulldown devices that are switched in Differential mode for most LS or FS signaling. One output is driven high when the other output is driven low to signal the J or K state. Both outputs are driven low for the LS or FS SE0 states. Both the USB_DP and USB_DM LS or FS pulldown drivers are also turned on simultaneously for HS signaling. This effect causes switching in both 45 Ω terminating resistors.
