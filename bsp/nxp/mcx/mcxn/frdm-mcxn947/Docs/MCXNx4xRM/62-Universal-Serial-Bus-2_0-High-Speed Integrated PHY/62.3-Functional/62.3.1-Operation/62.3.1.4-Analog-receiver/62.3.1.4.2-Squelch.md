## 62.3.1.4.2 Squelch detector

The squelch detector (also called envelope detector) is a differential analog receiver and threshold comparator. If the differentia magnitude is less than a nominal 100 mV threshold, its output is high (1b). Otherwise, its output is low (0b). The purpose of the squelch detector is to invalidate HS differential receiver when the incoming signal is too low to be received reliably.
