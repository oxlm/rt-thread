## 62.3.1.4.4 HS disconnect detector

The HS disconnect detector (also called the host disconnect detector) is a differential analog receiver and threshold comparator. If the differential magnitude between the USB_DP and USB_DM pins is greater than a nominal 575 mV threshold, its output is high (1b). Otherwise, its output is low (0b). The HS disconnect detector is only used in Host mode. The output of the detector is sampled during the long end-of-packet (EOP) that occurs at the end of the HS start-of-frame (SOF) packet, as enabled by CTRL[ENHOSTDISCONDETECT].
