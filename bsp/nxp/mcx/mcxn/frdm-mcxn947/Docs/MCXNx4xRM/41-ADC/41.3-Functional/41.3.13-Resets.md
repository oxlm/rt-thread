## 41.3.13 Resets


Table 366. ADC Resets


<table><tr><td>Reset source</td><td>Description</td></tr><tr><td>Chip reset</td><td>The logic and registers for ADC are reset to their default states on a chip reset.</td></tr><tr><td>Software reset</td><td>ADC implements a software reset field in its control register. CTRL[RST] resets all logic and registers to their default states, except for the CTRL register itself.</td></tr><tr><td>FIFO reset</td><td>ADC implements write-only control that resets FIFO0 (CTRL[RSTFIFO0]) and FIFO1 (CTRL[RSTFIFO1]). After a FIFO is reset, that FIFO is empty.</td></tr></table>
