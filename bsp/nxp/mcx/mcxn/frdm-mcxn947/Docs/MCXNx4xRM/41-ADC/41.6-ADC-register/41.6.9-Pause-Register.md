## 41.6.9 Pause Register (PAUSE)

Offset 

<table><tr><td>Register</td><td>Offset</td></tr><tr><td>PAUSE</td><td>24h</td></tr></table>

## Function

Controls the optional inserted delay between conversions. When CTRL[ADCEN] = 1, this register should not be modified.
